Incropper Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Incropper is a Windows app by Indilab Studio that straightens and enhances photos of documents. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Incropper works on your PC. It does not upload your images, does not need an account, and does not collect analytics. Indilab Studio does not receive your documents or your personal information through the app.

Images and documents

Images you open, paste, or capture are processed only on your PC. Incropper saves results only where you choose. It does not change your original files.

Clipboard and screen capture

Incropper reads the clipboard only when you paste or press its shortcut. It captures the screen only when you choose Capture screen area, and only the area you select. These images stay on your PC.

Information stored on your PC

Incropper keeps these items in its app data folder on your PC:

  • Your settings, such as save format and shortcuts
  • A list of up to 8 recently opened file paths
  • A local error log, which may contain technical details about a problem

This information is not sent to Indilab Studio. Uninstalling Incropper removes its app data from your PC.

Purchases

Incropper Pro is sold through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles payment and license information under its own privacy statement. Indilab Studio does not receive your payment details. Checking your Pro license may use a connection to the Microsoft Store.

Children

Incropper does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
